# CryptoSensei

_AI character teaches crypto investing through short videos and on-chain trading simulations_

## Summary

An AI-generated character explains investing and crypto basics in short-form videos for Instagram/TikTok, then invites viewers to play a risk-free trading simulation game on Solana. Users earn NFT badges as they complete lessons and hit simulated trading milestones, creating a shareable proof of learning.

## Target users

Investment beginners aged 20-35 who consume short video content on social media

## Problem

Investing education content is boring and passive; beginners have no safe way to practice before risking real money.

## Solution

Combine an engaging AI character video series with a gamified on-chain paper-trading simulator that rewards progress with collectible NFT badges.

## MVP features

- AI character video generator for crypto/investing lesson scripts (text-to-video pipeline)
- On-chain simulated trading game using real-time price feeds (mock SOL/USDC balances)
- NFT badge minting on Solana when users complete a lesson module or trading milestone
- Leaderboard ranking simulated portfolio returns among friends/followers
- Shareable short video clips auto-generated from user's simulation results for Instagram

## Chains

Solana

## Tech

Solana Web3.js, Anchor, Metaplex NFT, Pyth price feeds, ElevenLabs/TTS API, Remotion or FFmpeg for video generation, React/Next.js

## Category

Consumer

## Why now

Short-form video is the dominant content format for Gen Z, and Solana's low fees make frequent NFT minting for gamified learning economically viable.

## Roadmap

- Partner with crypto influencers to co-create AI character personas
- Add real yield/rewards for top simulated traders via sponsor pools
- Expand badge system into a composable on-chain reputation/credential layer
